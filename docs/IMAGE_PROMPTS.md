# Hero image prompts (ChatGPT Images)

Three photographs for the SwooshCV hero. Each one has a wide version for desktop and a tall
version for phones. Pick one of the three, make both sizes, and save them as:

| Size | Save as |
|---|---|
| Wide 16:9 | `web/public/hero/hero-wide.png` |
| Tall 9:16 | `web/public/hero/hero-tall.png` |

Make the wide and tall images from the same prompt so the light, colour and paper match.

## Rules for all three

- A photo, not an illustration or 3D render.
- Calm daylight with soft contrast. No lens flare, glow, sunset orange or purple.
- No text, letters, logos, people or hands.
- The centre stays empty. The headline and upload card sit there, so it has to be plain sky
  or plain surface with nothing sharp in it.
- Colours: pale sky blue, warm off-white, cool grey shadows. The only strong colour is the
  highlighter yellow in prompt C, which should be close to the site's `--color-changed`
  (`#f3ea7a`).

If ChatGPT gives you a 3:2 or 2:3 image, crop it to 16:9 or 9:16. Keep the empty centre and
crop from the edges.

## A. Paper airplane, high in the morning sky

The quietest of the three: mostly sky, and the plane is small.

### A wide (16:9)

```
Wide 16:9 landscape photograph of a soft early-morning sky. The sky fades from pale clear
blue at the top to a warm, hazy off-white near the bottom edge. A few thin, wispy cirrus
clouds drift low across the frame. One small white paper airplane, neatly folded from a
plain sheet of letter paper, glides in the upper-right third of the frame, angled gently
down toward the centre. Its creases are crisp, and the morning sun lights one wing while the
other wing has a soft cool-grey shadow. The plane is small, about 6% of the frame width,
with slight motion softness at the wing tips. The middle of the frame is completely empty,
calm sky with no clouds, birds or objects, leaving a wide clear area for a headline and a
form. Natural light only, shot on a 50mm lens, gentle film grain, high dynamic range, airy
and quiet like a premium product launch page. No text, no letters, no logos, no people, no
lens flare, no sun disc in frame, no vignette.
```

### A tall (9:16)

```
Tall 9:16 portrait photograph of a soft early-morning sky. The sky fades from pale clear
blue at the top to a warm, hazy off-white near the bottom edge. A few thin, wispy cirrus
clouds sit in the bottom fifth of the frame. One small white paper airplane, neatly folded
from a plain sheet of letter paper, glides in the top fifth of the frame, slightly right of

No text, no letters, no logos, no people, no lens flare, no sun disc in frame, no vignette.
```

## B. Paper airplane, closer, rising through haze

More of a scene: soft cloud banks below, and the plane is closer, so you can see it was
folded from a printed page.

### B wide (16:9)

```

The cloud bank fills the bottom quarter of the frame, lit warm white from the side by a low
morning sun out of frame to the left. Above it the sky is a clean, pale powder blue. One
white paper airplane, folded from a single printed page, rises from the lower-left toward
the upper-left, in the left quarter of the frame. It is close enough to show detail: crisp
folds, a slightly matte paper texture, and faint pale grey bands across the paper where
printed lines would be. The bands are fully blurred and abstract, with no readable
characters. Shallow depth of field, so the plane is sharp and the clouds are softly out of
focus. The centre and right half of the frame are open, empty sky, clear enough to place a
headline and a form on. Natural daylight, calm and optimistic, 85mm lens, subtle film grain,
soft contrast. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no people, no birds, no lens
flare, no dramatic sunset colours.
```

## C. Printed page, highlighter, window light

Goes with "invents nothing": one line on your own page gets marked, and nothing is added.

### C wide (16:9)

```
Wide 16:9 overhead photograph looking straight down at a pale, matte, light-oak desk in calm
morning window light. Soft shadows from a window frame fall diagonally across the desk from
the top-left, with long, gentle edges. A single printed white A4 page lies in the right
third of the frame, slightly rotated and partly cropped by the right edge. Its printed lines
are soft grey bars, slightly out of focus, with no legible letters. Exactly one line on the
page is highlighted with a clean stroke of pale lemon-yellow highlighter (close to #f3ea7a).
The stroke is slightly translucent and a little uneven at the ends, like real ink. A capped
yellow highlighter pen lies beside the page at a slight angle, near the bottom-right. The
left half and centre of the frame are bare desk surface with only soft light and shadow,
leaving a wide, quiet area for a headline and a form. Natural daylight, neutral white
balance leaning slightly warm, soft contrast, fine paper and wood texture, editorial still
life, subtle film grain. No readable text, no letters, no logos, no people, no hands, no
laptop, no coffee cup, no plants, no clutter.
```

- [ ] The wide and tall images look like the same morning.
